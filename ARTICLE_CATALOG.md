# Article Catalog — kontraktorepoxy.co.id

Scope: 102 evidence-led briefs for contractor-side planning, procurement, delivery, acceptance, and lifecycle management of resin-flooring projects in Indonesia.

Domain boundaries: deep epoxy chemistry is contextual material for `epoxy.co.id`; generic flooring taxonomy is contextual material for `lantai.id`; existing root service routes own commercial conversion. Cross-domain overlap is allowed. Cannibalization controls below apply only within `kontraktorepoxy.co.id`.

Catalog statistics: **17 topics × 6 briefs = 102 briefs**. The first wave contains 12 P0 assets. No brief is a city-name swap.

Priority legend: **P0** foundation/safety-critical launch asset; **P1** next connected cluster; **P2** later depth; **P3** maintainable historical context. Waves are bounded editorial packages, not instructions to publish everything at once.

## First-wave linking and measurement

`W1-foundation` is a bounded 12-asset project path: suitability → survey/moisture → requirements → preparation/details → application/K3 → ITP/tender → common failure diagnosis → contractor qualification. Each page links upward to its topic hub and laterally only to the next decision or evidence dependency.

Before release, require technical/HSE review, sources, diagrams/checklists, and explicit commercial-route separation. Monitor valid indexation and canonical selection, impressions by intent, checklist/tool use, educational-to-commercial navigation, qualified inquiries, assisted conversions, and GSC query/page pairs that signal same-domain cannibalization.
